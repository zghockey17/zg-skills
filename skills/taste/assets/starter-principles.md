# My taste: principles

> STARTER FILE. These are a generic starting point, not your taste yet.
> Setup keeps, edits, or deletes each one based on your answers and your own session evidence.
> Edit freely. Delete this note once the file is yours.

Each principle is a target plus the tell that you missed it.
Status after setup: `confirmed` (3+ sessions in the ledger), `stated` (you said it directly), or `starter` (unproven, kept on trust).

1. **One spacing scale, one rhythm.** `starter`
   Every margin, padding and gap comes from one scale; section breaks share one value; gaps within a row match; opening an accordion or tab moves nothing outside it.
   Do `gap: var(--s4)`. Tell: a seam that differs from its neighbours, a crunched block beside an airy one, white space nobody chose, layout shift on open or scroll.
2. **Simple.** `starter`
   One idea per section, one accent, one type family.
   Tell: eyebrow labels, numbered section labels ("01 / 02 / 03"), badges, chrome repeated on every slide, giant titles, bold on every other phrase. The usual word for the miss is _vibe-coded_.
3. **Text earns its place.** `starter`
   Keep a line only if it changes what the reader does or understands.
   Tell: a helper sentence under a heading, a subtitle restating the title, explanatory text at half opacity or in a third grey.
4. **Readable floors.** `starter`
   Body 16px or larger, captions 14px, meaningful icons 20px, all in full ink or the one muted tone.
   Tell: 11 to 12px labels carrying meaning.
5. **Motion is calm and slower than you think.** `starter`
   One easing for the piece; state changes short, storytelling beats longer; the page loads settled unless the entrance is the point; `prefers-reduced-motion` honored.
   Tell: pulse loops, bounce, staggered entrances competing for attention, a flash on first paint, motion that reruns on every re-render.
6. **Hover is one quiet change.** `starter`
   A hairline shadow or a 2 to 4 percent wash, in the same tone the selected or open state uses.
   Tell: a tinted accent fill, hover that moves layout, a marquee that stops instead of slowing.
7. **No accent strip.** `starter`
   Cards and buttons are a hairline ring plus at most a faint shadow; state shows as a wash or a ring.
   Tell: a thick colored left border, a colored top strip or corner, body background showing below the last section on overscroll.
8. **Small text stays human.** `starter`
   Dates, pills, chips, meta lines and labels use the body font, with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` where figures align. Monospace is for literal code, commands and paths.
   Tell: a mono date under a title, uppercase letter-spaced mono labels.
9. **Look before you report.** `starter`
   Screenshot the real render at desktop and phone width, scrolled end to end, plus hover and open states, and look at it.
   Tell: "done" reported from the code alone.
